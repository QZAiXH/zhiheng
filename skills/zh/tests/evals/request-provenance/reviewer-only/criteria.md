## Comparison boundary

These cases test task recording and standalone specification use, multi-turn changes, adopted external authority, historical gaps, and a read-only neighbor. They do not test external service permissions, secret redaction behavior, automated semantic validation, implementation quality, user-message discovery from inaccessible histories, or statistical reliability across repeated trials. This suite contains no redaction case; that branch needs a further synthetic case if adoption depends on measured redaction behavior.

A candidate adoption recommendation needs completed comparable forward runs plus independent Standards and Spec reviews of the exact pinned version. Stronger prose, static checks, or a successful single case is not evidence of net benefit. Identify regressions and unassessable criteria. Preserve original outputs and failures; do not coach an executor into a passing output.
